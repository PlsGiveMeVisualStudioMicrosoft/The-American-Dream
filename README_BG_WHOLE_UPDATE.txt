BELIZE–GUATEMALA WHOLE UPDATE
==============================

Core systems
------------
- Two-sided Belize–Guatemala tension system with opponent notification events.
- Belize metrics: International Support, Border Readiness, Public Confidence.
- Guatemala metrics: Claim Momentum, Military Pressure, Domestic Support.
- Active Guatemalan AI that normally escalates more often than it de-escalates.
- Threshold feedback at 40, 70 and 90 tension.
- Six-month Belize survival/ceasefire pressure mechanic while Belmopan remains held.

Balance
-------
- Belize begins with Defence of the Homeland: strong defence and entrenchment, weak attack.
- At 40 tension, Warn the People of the Imminent Crisis unlocks and spawns two 4-width,
  1,500-person territorial formations.
- Guatemala begins with the Petén Logistical Bottleneck and must develop logistics before
  fully exploiting its numerical superiority.
- Guatemala's OOB is represented by six genuine brigade/battlegroup-scale formations,
  approximately 20,000–23,000 personnel in total.

Foreign formations
------------------
- Every purchased formation is 4 combat width and approximately 1,000 personnel.
- Maximum active purchases through this system: five different contingents.
- The division characteristics are encoded in source-specific battalion types.
- Consumer-goods burdens are temporary and doubled from the first design proposal:
  Diaspora 10%, Jamaica 16%, Guyana 22%, Trinidad 24%, Honduras 30%, Dominican Republic 34%,
  El Salvador 38%, Cuba 44%, Nicaragua 48%, Mexico 56%, Colombia 90%.
- Colombia lasts 120 days; other contracts last 180 days.

Haiti reciprocity
-----------------
- During the Haitian crisis, Haiti may ask Belize for weapons or a BDF detachment while
  Belize–Guatemala tension remains below 40.
- Weapons are later repaid with weapons.
- If Belize sent troops, Haiti later sends the Haitian Solidarity Brigade once Haiti is at
  peace and Belize is at war with Guatemala.
- Haitian return brigade: four infantry battalions (8 width), engineer company, regular
  experience and 87% equipment.
