THE AMERICAN DREAM — BELIZE/GUATEMALA WAR & AI OVERHAUL
========================================================

IMPORTANT
---------
Start a NEW GAME when testing this build. Existing saves will not reliably receive
new starting divisions, restored victory points, locked templates, or the full AI
strategy setup.

CORE CHANGES
------------
1. Emergency force-generation decisions
   - Belize and Guatemala can raise real formations for political power only.
   - Decision-created formations enter at 1% equipment.
   - The remaining manpower and equipment must come from the country's real pools.
   - AI receives high decision priority and sensible division caps/cooldowns.

2. Army and template overhaul
   - Belize starts with four useful formations instead of tiny duplicate structures.
   - Guatemala starts with eight formations suited to the northern campaign.
   - Core templates are locked so the AI cannot decommission them.
   - The old focus-created free border-garrison OOB was removed.

3. Dedicated war AI
   - Guatemala concentrates divisions against Belize and executes offensive plans.
   - Belize prioritises the Guatemalan front and national defence.
   - Both AIs prioritise army construction and emergency recruitment decisions.

4. No early scripted white peace
   - The old six-month ceasefire escape has been removed.
   - A ceasefire can only be proposed after 18+ months, strong Belizean resistance,
     and severe Guatemalan political exhaustion.
   - Guatemala normally continues the campaign unless conditions are extreme.

5. Consolidated state modifiers
   - Belize: Defence Network, Mopan Line, Distributed Supply Network.
   - Guatemala: Petén Logistics, Campaign Support, Border Command.
   - Tiers replace one another instead of stacking a wall of near-identical icons.
   - Legacy modifier IDs remain only for save compatibility and are safely removed.

6. Focus reward rebalance
   - Major rifle/support-equipment giveaways were sharply reduced.
   - Military focuses now specialise in recruitment authority, manpower, logistics,
     command, fortifications, intelligence, or industry instead of repeating stockpiles.
   - Border forts were reduced to key positions rather than covering every province.

7. Belizean cities restored
   - Belize City, Belmopan, Corozal, Orange Walk, San Ignacio, Dangriga,
     Placencia, and Punta Gorda now have named victory points.

8. Continuous focus panel
   - Moved far away from the normal Belize and Guatemala focus trees.

9. Error cleanup included in this system
   - Missing decision categories defined.
   - Guatemala mountaineer technology key corrected.
   - Belize victory-point syntax corrected.
   - Known missing postwar focus icons replaced with valid generic icons.
   - Unsafe repeated dynamic-modifier removals replaced with guarded cleanup.
